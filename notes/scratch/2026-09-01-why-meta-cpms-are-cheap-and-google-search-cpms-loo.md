# Why Meta CPMs are cheap and Google Search CPMs look scary
date: 2026-09-01

Coming from Meta, a search CPM looks insane. It isn't — you are buying a different thing.

META = flyers on a busy street. The platform shows your ad to people who were NOT asking for it. CTR ~1-2%. Impressions are plentiful and individually near-worthless, so CPM is low ($10-40 typical). You are genuinely buying impressions, so CPM is a REAL metric there — watch it.

GOOGLE SEARCH = a queue of people who walked in and asked for you by name. An impression only exists because someone typed the query. Far fewer impressions, each one a person with live intent. CTR 20-45% on brand. Since CPM = CPC x CTR x 1000, a high CTR mechanically inflates CPM. L'ange Brand Search US: $0.55 CPC x 30.56% = $166.77 CPM — while being the CHEAPEST CPC in the whole account.

RULE: on Google Search, ignore CPM; read CPC, conversions, ROAS. On Meta, CPM is worth watching. Never compare the two numbers to each other.
