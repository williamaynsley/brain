# Two audiences, one budget — EquaVetz
date: 2026-08-28

EquaVetz sells two ranges to two completely different people.

The Trainer buys the racing range. Reached through Clare's relationships, vets, stable contracts. Can Meta even find a racehorse trainer in Victoria? Probably not reliably.

The Owner buys Daily Care. On Instagram, in equestrian Facebook groups, buys to fix something visible — an itch, a dull coat. That is a textbook paid-social buyer.

**The thought I keep circling:** the brief launches paid with the racing range because organic goes first. But paid and organic don't reach the same person. Sequencing paid to follow organic might be optimising the wrong constraint.
