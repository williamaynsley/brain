# Template — how to log a test
date: 2026-08-28

Copy this shape for every real test.

**Hypothesis** — what you believe and why.
**Prediction** — commit to a number BEFORE you look. This is the bit that teaches you.
**Setup** — what ran, where, how much, how long.
**Result** — what actually happened.
**Verdict** — were you right? If not, what was wrong with your model?

The prediction is non-negotiable. Reading a result you didn't predict teaches you almost nothing — you'll just decide you knew it all along.
